A PRIVATE STAFF WORKSPACE

Student records are never publicly searchable.

Only verified school personnel can sign in. Advisers are limited to one administrator-assigned section, and students do not have accounts or direct access to the system.

What it does

Clear tools for the full support workflow

Keep essential school records organized, access-controlled, and easy to review.

▤

Violation records

Record student violations with category, date and time, location, factual details, and an evidence reference. Each report receives a case number; adviser reports enter review. SVTS does not determine guilt or select sanctions.

♙

Student directory

Maintain the student information needed for school support and associate records with the correct school year and section.

◷

Community service

Assign service hours and days, record attendance, and track progress through completion with a verifiable history.

▦

Reports and exports

Filter student, violation, and community-service records, print every matching result, or export an Excel workbook with overall and section sheets.

⚙

School configuration

Administrators manage school years, sections, offense categories, and verified staff access.

◈

Accountability built in

Server-enforced permissions, protected sessions, and administrative audit history help keep access and changes accountable.

How it works

A careful path from setup to follow-up

Clear steps keep school records useful while decisions remain with staff.

1

Configure the school

An administrator sets up a school year, sections, offense categories, and staff access.

2

Record and review violations

Staff record factual details and supporting evidence references; each report receives a case number and is reviewed by authorized personnel.

3

Track service activity

4

Review and report

Authorized personnel review records and export administrative summaries when needed.

Questions

Frequently asked

How do advisers get an account?

Advisers request access with their school email. The request remains pending until an administrator assigns exactly one section and approves it. Advisers cannot sign in before approval.

Can students sign in or search records?

No. There are no student accounts, student portal, or public student-record lookup. Only verified school personnel can access records.

What can advisers access?

Advisers can view student and violation records for their one assigned section, submit new violation reports and register students only within that section, and perform permitted service-attendance tasks. Adviser reports enter Under Review; only administrators can update violation records or manage school settings.

How is community service progress calculated?

Progress is based on recorded attendance and the assigned service requirements. Completion is recorded only after the required time and days are met.

Can reports be filtered, printed, or exported?

Each record module includes advanced filters. Advisers can print matching records for their assigned section; administrators can export an Excel workbook with an overall sheet and section-specific sheets.

Where is the data stored?

The local installation stores data in a SQLite database on the server. Protect the server and database, keep secure backups, and use an HTTPS-enabled production deployment before any public exposure.

Access the secure staff workspace

Advisers and administrators sign in with verified school accounts. Adviser access requires administrator approval.
